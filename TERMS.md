# Terms of Service - Crocodile King

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Crocodile King is a free community host bot for THE SWAMP Discord server, run by the Swampology.com community owners. He greets members, helps with verification reminders, runs quests and games, and keeps XP and level books for the server. By using THE SWAMP and interacting with Crocodile King, you agree to these terms.

## 1. A free entertainment system

Crocodile King is a free entertainment and community system. There is no charge to use him, and no purchase is needed to earn XP, levels, or Swamp Bucks.

## 2. Virtual points have no cash value

XP, levels, titles, and Swamp Bucks are virtual only. They have no cash value, they are not money, and they cannot be exchanged or transferred for money or anything outside the server. Server admins may adjust, correct, or reset XP, levels, Swamp Bucks, streaks, and roles at any time, for example to fix a bug, undo farming, or restart a season.

## 2a. Items, Raid Passes, factions, passes and action points

Members who boost the server receive a monthly Booster Pack of virtual items (Swamp Bucks, a Raid Pass, loot and reputation) for as long as their boost is active, once per calendar month. Loot items - including Raid Passes - are virtual inventory items with no cash value, exactly like XP and Swamp Bucks. A Raid Pass can be spent in the bot to trigger a server-wide event; it is not a ticket to anything outside the server. Faction membership is a team label inside the server's games; switching factions can cost virtual Swamp Bucks and has a cooldown, both set by the bot's rules. A VIP Pass is either a server role or a timed grant given by server admins inside the bot; while active it raises a member's daily action points and adds a bonus XP line, and it ends when its time runs out. Action points are a daily play allowance for certain bot games: they refill each day and have no value outside the bot. Cosmetic titles are display text shown inside the bot only.

## 2b. The King's store, consumables and decorations

The bot includes a store (The King's Little Store) where members spend Swamp Bucks - and only Swamp Bucks. Swamp Bucks cannot be bought with real money anywhere in the bot, and real money is never accepted for store goods. Everything in the store is virtual and has no cash value: loot items, a one-day pass, an XP feast, single-use consumables (a Streak Shield that protects a daily streak once, a Double-Down Token that doubles the Swamp Bucks of one quest claim, and an Event Lure that triggers an event), and decorations. Members may also sell loot items back to the store; selling pays Swamp Bucks only, never money. Avatar frames and profile backgrounds are decorations: buying one adds it to the member's in-bot wardrobe, and the equipped background and frame decorate the member's profile card inside the bot. The bot can also generate a picture file that composites the member's own current Discord avatar inside a purchased frame; the member may download that file and upload it as their own Discord avatar. The bot cannot and does not modify anyone's Discord account or profile, and it does not grant or sell Discord's own avatar decorations or shop items - only the member can change their own Discord avatar.

## 3. Fair play

Play straight. Do not spam, use bots or scripts, or use alternate accounts to farm XP, Swamp Bucks, quest rewards, or event prizes. Admins and mods may remove XP, Swamp Bucks, levels, or roles gained by farming or cheating, and may limit or remove a member's access to bot features for breaking this rule.

## 4. Sponsor quests

Some quests are sponsored. Every sponsor quest is always clearly labelled "Sponsored". Taking part is always optional. Sponsor quest prizes are virtual (XP and Swamp Bucks) unless the quest's own text says otherwise. A sponsor quest may also award a server role, or a digital prize key provided by the sponsor; keys are delivered to the winning member by direct message only and are never posted in a public channel. Sponsors receive participation counts only, never member lists or private information.

## 5. Changes to the bot

The owners and admins may change, pause, or remove any bot feature, quest, event, reward, or bonus at any time, with or without notice.

Crocodile King is provided "as is", without warranties of any kind. The owners are not responsible for lost XP, Swamp Bucks, streaks, or progress if data is lost, for example if the hosting service resets its storage, or for downtime while the bot is offline.

Using Crocodile King also means following Discord's own Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. If Discord or the server admins remove a member from THE SWAMP, their bot record may be removed too.
