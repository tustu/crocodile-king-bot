# Privacy Policy - Crocodile King

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Crocodile King is a free community host bot for THE SWAMP Discord server, run by the Swampology.com community owners. This policy says plainly what the bot stores, why, and how to have your record deleted.

## What the bot stores

For each member who uses THE SWAMP, the bot stores a record tied to their Discord user ID with only these things:

- XP, level, and Swamp Bucks balance
- Daily streak and the date of the last daily claim
- The date the member joined the server
- Whether the member is verified, the date they were verified, and which verification reminder stage they have reached
- Quest progress for the day (counts of messages, voice minutes, reactions, Swamp Art posts, and correct trivia answers) and which day's quest was claimed
- Activity counters: total messages counted, total voice minutes, and a timestamp used for the message XP cooldown
- Sponsor quest award records: member user ID and the time of the award, nothing else
- Faction membership, and the member's faction XP contributions counted into weekly and all-time faction pools
- Reputation (Rep) score
- Loot inventory: which virtual items the member holds and how many of each
- Cosmetic titles the member has unlocked or had generated, and which one is active
- VIP Pass state: whether a timed pass is active and the time it expires
- Action points left for the day and the date they belong to
- Generated side quest state: the member's current side quest (type, target and progress) until it is claimed or dropped
- Sponsor-provided digital prize keys for a sponsor quest, held in the quest's key pool only until they are awarded to a winning member by direct message
- For server boosters, the month (year and month only) of their last Booster Pack, used only to pay the pack once per month
- Store state: the date (day only) of the member's last Firefly Tonic purchase, which avatar frames and profile backgrounds the member owns and which are equipped, and how many store consumables (Streak Shields, Double-Down Tokens, Event Lures) the member holds

The bot does not collect or store email addresses, real names, payment information, or location.

## Why it is stored

This information is used only to run the bot's own features: working out XP, levels, and XP bonuses, paying daily and quest rewards, tracking streaks, sending verification reminders, running trivia and Gator Ate My Answer games, keeping faction standings, reputation, loot inventories, cosmetic titles, action points and generated side quests, running the Swamp Bucks store (balances are never charged in real money - no payment information exists to store), and counting sponsor quest participation. Sponsor prize keys are used for one thing only: delivery to the member who won them.

## Message content

The bot reads message text in the moment only, to count activity and to check trivia and Gator Ate My Answer guesses against the answer. Message text is not stored, not logged to the data file, and never sold or shared.

## Voice

Voice activity is counted as minutes only: the bot counts a member who is sitting in a voice channel as present for that minute. No audio is recorded, listened to, or stored, ever.

## Where the data lives

Records are kept in a local data file on the machine that hosts the bot (data/store.json). It is not kept in an outside database service.

## Sharing

Personal data is not sold, rented, or shared. Sponsors of sponsor quests receive aggregate participation counts only (for example, how many awards were given in a month). They never receive member lists, user records, or any private information.

## Deleting your data

Any member can ask for their bot record to be deleted. Contact a server admin or mod in THE SWAMP Discord server and ask them to remove your Crocodile King record; your entry will be removed from the bot's data file. Deleting the record removes your XP, levels, Swamp Bucks, and streak permanently.

## Children

Discord's own age rules apply to THE SWAMP and to this bot. Anyone who is not old enough to use Discord under Discord's Terms of Service should not use the server or the bot.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page and the "Last updated" date at the top will change.

## Questions

Questions about this policy or your data: ask a server admin or mod in THE SWAMP Discord server.
